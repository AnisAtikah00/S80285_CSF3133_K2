About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially chartered on 1 February 2007 and specializes in marine science, oceanography, maritime studies, and natural resource management.

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu began in 1979 as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu. Over the decades, it evolved into a branch campus, gained autonomy as Terengganu University College (KUT) in 2001, and was eventually elevated to full-fledged university status as UMT.
